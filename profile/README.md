## 🌟 About Us

**Idea and Innovation Cell (IIC)** is the premier technical and innovation society of **Parala Maharaja Engineering College (PMEC), Berhampur**.

We are a vibrant community of **developers, designers, thinkers, and innovators** dedicated to fostering a strong culture of:

- 🚀 Product development
- 💡 Problem solving
- 🌐 Open-source collaboration
- 🎨 Design and creativity
- 🤖 Emerging technologies
- 🤝 Knowledge sharing

Our goal is to provide students with a platform where **ideas can transform into real-world solutions**.

---

## 🎯 What We Do

- 💻 **Open-Source & Project Development**  
  Building scalable web applications, mobile tools, automation systems, and technology solutions for campus and community needs.

- 💡 **Workshops & Bootcamps**  
  Conducting hands-on learning sessions in Full-Stack Web Development, AI/ML, Cloud Computing, App Development, and other emerging technologies.

- 🎨 **Design & Creative Tech**  
  Exploring UI/UX design, visual branding, wireframing, prototyping, and human-centered design.

- 🏆 **Hackathons & Competitions**  
  Organizing hackathons, ideathons, coding challenges, and innovation-driven competitions to showcase student talent.

- 🤝 **Mentorship & Networking**  
  Connecting students with alumni, industry professionals, technical experts, and fellow innovators.
