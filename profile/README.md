## 🌟 About Us

**Idea and Innovation Cell (IIC)** is the premier technical and innovation society of **Parala Maharaja Engineering College (PMEC), Berhampur**. We are a vibrant community of developers, designers, thinkers, and innovators dedicated to fostering a strong culture of product development, problem solving, and open-source collaboration.

---

## 🎯 What We Do

- 💻 **Open-Source & Project Development:** Building scalable web applications, mobile tools, and automated systems for campus and community needs.
- 💡 **Workshops & Bootcamps:** Conducting hands-on training in Full-Stack Web Development, AI/ML, Cloud Computing, and App Development.
- 🎨 **Design & Creative Tech:** UI/UX prototyping, visual branding, and human-centered design.
- 🏆 **Hackathons & Competitions:** Organizing flagship hackathons, ideathons, and coding challenges to showcase student talent.
- 🤝 **Mentorship & Networking:** Connecting students with alumni, industry leaders, and technical experts.

🤝 Contributing & Getting Involved
We welcome contributions from students, developers, and open-source enthusiasts:

Fork any project repository.

Create a branch for your feature or bug fix:

Bash
git checkout -b feature/NewFeature
Commit your changes with clear messages:

Bash
git commit -m 'Add NewFeature'
Push to your branch:

Bash
git push origin feature/NewFeature
Open a Pull Request for the team to review!

🚀 Bring Your Project to Idea & Innovation Cell!
Have you built an interesting project, tool, or website? We invite all PMECians and student developers to feature their work under the Idea and Innovation Cell organization!

💡 Ways to Get Your Project Featured:
Submit Your Repository to IIC:

Have an existing project you'd like to open-source or incubate under IIC?

Open an Issue or submit a Pull Request adding your repository details to our projects list, or reach out to transfer your repository under the Idea-Innovation-Cell GitHub organization.

Contribute to Existing Projects:

Browse any repository under our organization.

Pick an issue or propose a new feature.

Fork, build, and submit a Pull Request (PR) — all valid contributions get reviewed and merged!

Pitch a New Project Idea:

Want to build something from scratch with a team? Open a Discussion / Issue or mail us at ideainnovationcell.pmec@gmail.com with your project roadmap!
